About

I work at the point where offensive security meets applied machine learning — spending part of my time thinking like an attacker, and the rest teaching systems to notice one. My background runs from digital forensics and penetration testing to building AI models that catch what signature-based tools miss, like malware that never touches disk. I care about security that's grounded in how real attackers operate, not checklists — and about AI that's built to be trusted under pressure, not just accurate on a benchmark.
